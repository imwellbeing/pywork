        Who is Olivia Rodrigo?

        Olivia Rodrigo is an American singer-songwriter who grew up in Temecula, California. Before music took over, she was known as an actor on Disney Channel’s Bizaardvark and later as Nini in High School Musical: The Musical: The Series.

        In January 2021 she released “drivers license,” a piano ballad about a painful breakup. It debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 and turned her into a global name almost overnight. Her debut album, SOUR, followed that May, with Dan Nigro producing most of it.

        At the 2022 Grammys she won Best New Artist, Best Pop Vocal Album and Best Pop Solo Performance.

        About GUTS

        After SOUR made her a star, Olivia Rodrigo came back with GUTS on September 8, 2023. Dan Nigro produced it again, and the album debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200.

        The lead single, “vampire,” arrived in June 2023 and debuted at No. 1 on the Hot 100. The record keeps the diary-style songwriting but adds more humor and more pop-punk energy, and it spends a lot of time on the pressure of being young, perfect and judged.

        A softer, dreamier record that follows one relationship from the first butterflies to the heartbreak.

        About the album

        Olivia Rodrigo released her third album on June 12, 2026, on Geffen Records. Dan Nigro produced most of it again, with extra help from Jim-E Stack, Noah Conrad and Mike Wise.

        It swaps the guitar crunch of her earlier records for synths and ’80s new wave, and Robert Smith of The Cure is the only guest. The 13 songs are split into two sides, seven called “girl so in love” and six called “you seem pretty sad.” In the UK, it gave her the biggest opening week of her career.
